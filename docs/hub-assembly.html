Assemble the ClickTapToe hub

The enclosure and wheel STEP files are being revised.

Parts

  • Printed hub body, cover, and wheel. Shaft, two 608RS bearings, and a diametrically magnetized magnet.
  • Populated hub board, AS5600 magnetic sensor, four spacers, and a STEMMA QT / Qwiic cable with 4-pin JST-SH plugs at both ends.
  • Heat-set inserts, mounting screws, wheel set screw, two ¼-inch TS jack nuts, and optional ½ oz tire weights.
  • Assembled slipper, straight-through RJ45 cable, two click pedals, and a USB-C data cable.

Tools

Soldering iron with an insert tip, 1.5 mm and 2.5 mm hex drivers, and a wrench for the jack nuts.

Steps

  1. Install the bearings
  2. Controller board inserts
  3. Magnetic sensor inserts
  4. Rear cover inserts
  5. Scroll wheel insert
  6. Wheel and shaft
  7. Mount the magnetic sensor
  8. Mount the controller board
  9. Connect the sensor cable
  10. Add the tire weights
  11. Install the rear cover
  12. Install the TS jack nuts
  13. Connect and test the slipper

Step 1 Install the two bearings

  • Use two 608RS sealed ball bearings. Each has an 8 mm inner diameter, a 22 mm outer diameter, and a 7 mm width.
  • Find the two round bearing seats at the ends of the scroll wheel slot. One is visible from above, and the other lines up with the opening in the wall of the electronics bay.
  • Press on the outer ring of each bearing to seat it squarely in its recess.

Step 2 Install the controller board inserts

  • Set the hub body down with the electronics bay facing you. Find the four raised mounting posts, one in each corner of the bay floor.
  • Fit one M3 × 4 × 5 mm heat-set insert in each post. Use the heated tip of a soldering iron to press each insert in straight until it is flush with the top of the post.
  • Let the plastic cool.

Step 3 Install the magnetic scroll sensor inserts

  • Find the four small holes around the shaft opening on the inner wall. They line up with the magnetic scroll sensor's mounting holes.
  • Use the soldering iron to seat one M2 × 4 × 3.5 mm heat-set insert in each hole.

Step 4 Install the rear cover inserts

  • Find the small hole at the lower end of each side rail.
  • Use the soldering iron to seat one M2 × 4 × 3.5 mm heat-set insert in each hole. These inserts hold the rear cover.

Step 5 Install the scroll wheel insert

  • Find the small hole in the tread of the scroll wheel.
  • Heat-set an M3 × 4 × 5 mm insert into the tread. Press it in straight until it reaches the bottom of the hole, then let the plastic cool.

Step 6 Install the scroll wheel and shaft

  • Lower the wheel into the slot and line up its center hole with both bearings. Slide the shaft in from the electronics bay, through the wheel, and into the opposite bearing.
  • Center the wheel in the slot. Thread the set screw (an M3 × 20 mm hex socket head cap screw) into the tread insert. Tighten it with a 2.5 mm hex driver until it holds the wheel on the shaft.
  • Turn the wheel by hand to check that it spins freely with the screw head clear of the enclosure.

Step 7 Mount the magnetic scroll sensor

  • Center the diametrically magnetized magnet on the end of the shaft facing the electronics bay.
  • Place four spacers between the inner wall and the AS5600 board. Secure the board with four M2 × 8 mm hex socket head cap screws.
  • Check that the sensor is directly opposite the magnet and that the magnet can turn without touching the board.

Step 8 Install the controller board

  • With USB disconnected, place the populated input hub board on its four mounting posts so its ports align with the rear cover openings.
  • Secure the board to the posts with four M3 × 4 mm hex socket head cap screws.

Step 9 Wire the scroll sensor to the hub board

  • With USB disconnected, plug one end of the STEMMA QT / Qwiic cable into the magnetic scroll sensor.
  • Plug the other end into the hub board socket marked J5. Each plug only fits its socket one way.
  • Route the cable away from the spinning wheel and the cover edges.

Step 10 Add the tire weights

  • Add as many ½ oz tire weights as you need to the two pockets next to the wheel to reach your desired weight.
  • Peel off the backing to stick the weights in place, or leave them loose so you can adjust the weight later.

Step 11 Install the rear cover

  • Fit the curved rear cover into the side rails. Keep the sensor cable clear and center the four ports in their openings.
  • Install two M2 × 4 mm hex socket head cap screws, one at each lower corner of the rear panel.

Step 12 Install the TS jack nuts

  • From outside the cover, thread a retaining nut onto each ¼-inch TS pedal jack marked L and R.
  • Tighten each nut until it is snug against the cover. Hold the jack steady so it does not twist.

Step 13 Connect the slipper and test

  • With USB power disconnected, plug a short straight-through RJ45 cable into the hub port marked SLIPPER and into the matching port on the assembled slipper.
  • Plug normally closed click pedals into L and R, then connect the hub to the computer with a USB-C data cable. If the controller needs firmware, follow the firmware flashing steps.
  • In a browser window, check that the left pedal clicks and the right pedal opens a context menu. Turn the wheel in both directions to test scrolling, then slide the slipper to test pointer movement.
