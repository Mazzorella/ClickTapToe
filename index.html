A mouse you control with your feet.

ClickTapToe is a USB mouse that shifts everyday pointing, clicking, and scrolling away from your hands.

Read the assembly guide → See how it works

How it works

Click

Press one pedal for a left click and the other for a right click.

Scroll

Roll the hub’s large wheel with your foot to scroll up or down.

Move the pointer

Slide the slipper across the floor to move the pointer on screen.

Assemble the hub

Follow the photos from fitting the bearings to connecting the slipper. The enclosure and wheel STEP files are being revised.
